# App Store Listing Copy

## App Name
CaPowHr

## Subtitle
BLE Indoor Workouts for Watch

## Promotional Text
Record indoor bike, treadmill, and rower workouts on Apple Watch with power, cadence, heart rate, distance, and calories saved to Apple Health.

## Description
CaPowHr is the Apple Watch app for athletes whose indoor workouts live on Bluetooth gym equipment.

Connect FTMS smart bikes, treadmills, and rowers — plus cycling power meters and cadence sensors — directly from your wrist. CaPowHr records accurate power, cadence, heart rate, distance, and calories to Apple Health without requiring your iPhone during the workout.

**Why CaPowHr**
- Standalone Apple Watch operation for gym and home setups
- Supports bikes, treadmills, and rowers via FTMS
- Intelligent heart rate source selection (equipment, watch, or auto)
- Multi-strategy distance tracking when equipment data varies
- Optional Strava upload after each workout
- Trusted device reconnect for daily training
- Pause, lap splits, training zones, and structured workout templates
- FIT export for power users
- Watch complications for quick starts

**Built for indoor athletes**
Apple Fitness is great for many workouts, but it does not capture external power meters, FTMS equipment metrics, or CSC-based distance the way dedicated BLE recorders can. CaPowHr fills that gap.

**Health data**
Workouts save to Apple Health with heart rate, power, cadence, distance, speed, and active energy where available.

**Companion app**
Use the optional iPhone companion to connect Strava when watch-only OAuth is inconvenient.
